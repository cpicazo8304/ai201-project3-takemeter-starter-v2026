Reasoned Opinion: a judgment about a song or album that gives a reason, explanation, or evidence based on lyrics, instrumentation, melody, vocal delivery, vibe, or overall musical quality.
Unreasoned Opinion: a direct verdict about a song or album’s quality without explaining why; a quick claim or rating such as “this is great” or “this is bad.”
Emotional Reaction: a reaction driven by feeling or tone—excitement, humor, frustration, affection, or surprise—without offering a clear reasoned judgment about the song’s quality.
